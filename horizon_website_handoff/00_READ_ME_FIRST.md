# HORIZON — Website Handoff Pack

## Purpose

This folder is the working source of truth for building the first public website for **Horizon**.

Horizon is currently an early-stage future-living initiative and long-term company vision. The website should communicate ambition, seriousness, technical curiosity, and design maturity **without pretending that Horizon has already built projects that do not yet exist**.

The first site should function as:

1. a public expression of the Horizon vision;
2. a founder portfolio project;
3. an evolving home for research, prototypes, simulations, and future built work;
4. a credible artifact that can be shared with universities, scholarship programmes, researchers, engineers, developers, investors, architects, and potential collaborators;
5. the digital foundation for Horizon as the initiative grows.

## Core public positioning

**Horizon explores how intelligence can move beyond screens and into the places people live.**

It brings together:

- artificial intelligence and data;
- IoT, sensors, edge computing, and automation;
- smart energy, solar, batteries, and distributed systems;
- water resilience and monitoring;
- connectivity and digital infrastructure;
- climate-responsive buildings and indoor comfort;
- mobility and EV-ready infrastructure;
- human-centred architecture and community design;
- digital twins, simulation, and optimization;
- long-term research into materials, robotics, construction technology, and physical AI.

The intended progression is:

**one intelligent space → intelligent homes → connected communities → intelligent districts → future cities.**

Horizon begins with an **Africa-first perspective**, while the long-term ambition is global.

## Brand architecture

- **Horizon** — parent brand and overall vision
- **Horizon Living** — intelligent homes, hospitality, and human experience
- **Horizon Systems** — AI, IoT, data, automation, energy and infrastructure systems
- **Horizon Labs** — research, prototyping, simulation, R&D and experimentation
- **Horizon Communities** — connected residential developments and shared infrastructure
- **Horizon Cities** — long-term city-scale planning, infrastructure and intelligent urban systems

These are currently a **brand and capability roadmap**, not a claim that all are legally registered subsidiaries or already-operating business units.

## Site tone

The site must feel:

- elegant
- premium
- calm
- precise
- architectural
- intelligent
- optimistic
- cinematic
- African-first but globally credible
- futuristic without becoming science-fiction gimmickry

The site must **not** feel:

- like a crypto website;
- like a generic SaaS landing page;
- like a property listing portal;
- like a conventional construction company;
- overly neon/cyberpunk;
- crowded with cards;
- full of fake statistics, invented partnerships, or unsupported claims.

## Primary tagline

**Intelligent spaces. Brighter lives.**

Secondary language that can be used selectively:

- **A smarter, more human tomorrow.**
- **Building toward a brighter tomorrow.**
- **From intelligent spaces to future cities.**
- **Africa first. Beyond tomorrow.** — use sparingly; do not over-brand every sentence.

## Important truthfulness rule

Horizon is early-stage.

Use labels such as:

- `Concept`
- `Research`
- `Exploration`
- `Prototype in development`
- `Coming soon`
- `Long-term vision`

Never imply that Horizon currently owns land, has active city developments, has deployed infrastructure, has paying clients, or has formal partners unless those facts are later provided and verified.

## Recommended implementation order

1. Build the brand system and visual shell.
2. Build the homepage with real copy.
3. Add Horizon ecosystem / divisions.
4. Add Vision and Africa-first sections.
5. Add Research & Prototype area with honest `Coming soon` status.
6. Add founder perspective.
7. Add custom media/video components.
8. Add secondary pages only if they improve clarity.
9. Deploy to Vercel.
10. Iterate as Horizon research and prototypes become real.

Read all other documents in this folder before implementation.
