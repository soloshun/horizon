# HORIZON — Website Structure and Copy

This document provides the recommended V1 information architecture and initial website copy.

The copy is intentionally concise.

Do not add large amounts of generic marketing text merely to fill space.

---

# Recommended V1 structure

A polished one-page homepage can carry most of the story, with optional dedicated routes for Research and Vision.

Recommended routes:

```text
/
 /vision
 /research
 /about
```

Optional later:

```text
/journal
/projects
/collaborate
```

Do not create empty pages just to make the site look large.

## SECTION 03 — ONE SYSTEM

Label:
`AN INTEGRATED VIEW`

Headline:
# A home is more than a building.  
# A city is more than infrastructure.

Short body:
The future of living sits at the intersection of multiple systems.

Use an elegant interactive system map, not feature cards.

Nodes:

- Intelligence
- Energy
- Water
- Connectivity
- Buildings
- Mobility
- Environment
- Community

On interaction, show one sentence each.

### Intelligence
AI, forecasting, automation, digital twins and edge computing.

### Energy
Solar, batteries, smart distribution, load visibility and resilient power.

### Water
Monitoring, storage, efficiency, leak detection and resilience.

### Connectivity
Reliable digital infrastructure linking people, devices and services.

### Buildings
Climate-responsive, efficient spaces designed around human comfort.

### Mobility
EV-ready infrastructure, walkability and future transport systems.

### Environment
Air quality, climate awareness, materials and responsible resource use.

### Community
Technology designed to strengthen rather than replace human connection.

# ABOUT PAGE

Keep short.

## Suggested copy

# Horizon begins as a question.

**Can the places we live become more intelligent, resilient and human at the same time?**

Horizon is an early-stage initiative focused on future living, physical AI and intelligent environments.

Its founder comes from an AI and data background and is using Horizon as a long-term research and building journey into smart buildings, infrastructure, energy and cities.

The work begins with research and prototypes, with an Africa-first perspective and a global horizon.

Include:
- founder photo later;
- short technical background;
- current learning/research themes;
- contact.

Avoid a long CV on this page.

---

# Language rules

Prefer:
- "explores"
- "researches"
- "is developing"
- "aims to"
- "long-term vision"
- "future capability"

Use "builds" only for things that actually exist later.

Avoid:
- revolutionary
- world-leading
- cutting-edge
- game-changing
- disruptive
- best-in-class
- unprecedented

Let the work make those judgments.
