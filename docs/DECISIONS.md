# V1 decisions and open details

- Framework: standard Next.js App Router, directly in the main directory, with Vercel as the intended target. The supplied technical handoff and the user’s explicit Next.js/Vercel preference take precedence over the available Sites scaffold.
- Cost: no required third-party runtime API, paid CMS, database, analytics or email-delivery service. The enquiry flow produces an email draft. The Blender model and video were rendered locally. ElevenLabs remains unused.
- Theme: warm paper ivory and restrained gold, with an optional deep charcoal-green and gold dark theme. Device preference is respected initially and user choice persists.
- Typography: Manrope carries the identity and interface. Cormorant Garamond is used selectively for an editorial emphasis; it does not replace the original geometric brand direction.
- Brand: lightweight provisional SVG interpretations follow the supplied parent/division references. They are not represented as professionally finalized vector identity files. The supplied raster originals are unchanged.
- Founder: Solomon Eshun, based on the supplied personal photo, approved Gmail and user context. The founder page stays short and describes a personal, independent effort. No qualifications, employers or awards have been invented.
- Portrait: an on-page crop hides the IndabaX banner without altering Solomon’s likeness. The original `public/images/solo1.webp` is intact. Reframing uses explicit source coordinates and is shared by both portrait placements.
- H1: an actual editable Blender massing model and eight-second film were created. The site calls them a concept, never a functional home prototype. Its first study note describes the produced artifact and open questions.
- Contact: `solomoneshun373@gmail.com`, explicitly supplied by the user. No email was sent while building or testing.
- Hosting: no Vercel account or project is linked in this workspace. Production domain and deployment remain to be configured.
- Future evidence: add measured results, hardware, research sources and build logs only as they exist. Keep concept, simulation, prototype and deployment distinct.
